<section class="section" id="focus" aria-labelledby="focus-title">
  <div class="section-head"><h2 id="focus-title">Research Focus</h2></div>
  <p class="section-intro">Understanding how ground conditions, rainfall, and human activity influence slope stability.</p>
  <dl class="focus-list">
    <div><dt>Subsurface conditions</dt><dd>Weathered rock, weak layers, and water pathways that control slope failure.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Rainfall and human activity</dt><dd>The effects of rainfall, traffic, construction, and land-use change on fragile slopes.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Landslide mapping</dt><dd>Combining field evidence and spatial models to identify landslide-prone terrain.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Mountain infrastructure</dt><dd>Assessing landslide impacts on roads and railways to inform mitigation.</dd></div>
  </dl>
