**Engineering geologist specialising in multi-trigger slope failure mechanisms and their impact on mountain infrastructure resilience.**

I'm a PhD candidate at the [Centre for Disaster Mitigation and Management](https://vit.ac.in/centers/cdmm/), [Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT)](https://vit.ac.in/), under [Dr. G. P. Ganapathy](https://vitdirectory.vit.ac.in/page/faculty/11345). My doctoral research covers the geophysical and geospatial dimensions of landslide dynamics: natural causative factors and vehicle-induced ground vibrations along landslide-transportation corridors in the Nilgiris, Southern India.

I hold an M.Sc. in Applied Geology from the University of Madras and a B.Sc. in Geology from Thiruvalluvar University. I've also worked as a Teaching cum Research Assistant at VIT, and interned in earthquake geophysics at the Wadia Institute of Himalayan Geology and in hydrogeology at the Central Ground Water Board.

**Research goals:**

- Understand why rainfall-triggered landslides happen
- Improve landslide susceptibility mapping
- Quantify landslide impacts on critical infrastructure
- Grow into geological hazards more broadly, with a focus on application-oriented research
