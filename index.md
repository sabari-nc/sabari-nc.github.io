Welcome!
======
I specialize in monitoring landslides and assessing slope stability within mountainous regions prone to anthropogenic impacts. My research focuses on the use of geophysical techniques, particularly ground pulsation studies, to analyze and address slope instability. I also contribute to the development of mitigation strategies, including bioengineering methods and various engineering practices aimed at sustainable land management.

In addition, I’ve conducted research in micropaleontology (Foraminifera) and environmental geochemistry, with an interdisciplinary approach to understanding the Earth's processes.
